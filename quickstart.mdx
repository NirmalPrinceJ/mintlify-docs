The IntegrateWise onboarding does not just "create a workspace." It resolves a **Spine Context** — who you are, the org you're in, the reality you connect, and the depth at which you see it — then hydrates the Spine from your real data before the workbench opens.

## Prerequisites

- An invite or signup at [app.integratewise.ai](https://app.integratewise.ai).
- Admin access on at least one tool you plan to connect (CRM, support, finance, or docs).

## The four onboarding steps

The L0 flow walks you through four UI steps that produce a single `OnboardingData` object.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Authenticate with your work email. The gateway issues an RS256 JWT and establishes your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile">
    Pick your use case (`personal`, `work`, or `business`), department, industry, and company size. These resolve to a canonical **domain** (for example `CUSTOMER_SUCCESS`, `BIZOPS`, `SALES`) and an operating context (`CTX_CS`, `CTX_BIZOPS`, `CTX_SALES`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goals">
    Name your workspace and set the goal you want IntegrateWise to drive toward. This becomes part of your Spine Context, not a label on a folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Authorize one or more tools through Nango. Each connector is tagged Flow A (structured), B (unstructured), or C (AI interactions). Your first connector kicks off Spine hydration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens behind the scenes

While you watch the loader, the system runs eight constitutional phases:

1. **Identity** — gateway credential auth, RS256 JWT.
2. **Organization discovery** — `USE_CASE_DOMAINS` and `DEPARTMENT_CTX_MAP` resolve a canonical domain and `CTX_*`.
3. **Reality connections** — each authorized tool starts streaming via the Loader.
4. **Spine hydration** — the 8-stage pipeline classifies, normalizes, and writes the **Spine** (D1 domain partitions). The workbench arrives pre-filled.
5. **Memory activation** — conversational, org, and personal memory begin accumulating through the continuity pipeline.
6. **Projection generation** — your Spine Context yields nav, modules, entity views, and metrics.
7. **Governance activation** — the HARD GATE is live. AI may think and propose, but never act without an `x-approval-token`.
8. **Continuity activation** — the IW Continuity Bridge is on; the same context is shared across every AI, tool, and session.

The visible path is budgeted to about two minutes (`STEP_TIMINGS`). Spine hydration runs as a tracked job (`creamyJobId`) so the desk is populated, not blank.

## After onboarding

When you land on your workbench:

- The Spine already holds your normalized accounts, contacts, deals, tickets, tasks, and documents.
- The Twin (⌘J overlay) starts surfacing signals — renewal risks, deal slips, ticket spikes — with evidence.
- Every write-back to a connected tool waits for your approval in the Approval Center.
