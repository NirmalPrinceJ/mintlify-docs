IntegrateWise onboarding does not assign you a blank workspace. It builds a **Spine Context** for you — the shared, normalized truth across your tools — and projects a workbench from it. The moment a connector is authorized, real data starts flowing in.

## Prerequisites

- A work email and an account at [app.integratewise.ai](https://app.integratewise.ai).
- Admin or OAuth access to at least one of the tools you want to connect (CRM, support, finance, messaging, documents).

## The four-step flow

The visible onboarding has four steps and is budgeted to ~2 minutes. Spine hydration continues in the background so your desk arrives pre-filled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Sign in and confirm the account you want to onboard. Identity is established here (gateway auth, RS256 JWT) — this is the user side of the Spine Context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile">
    Tell IntegrateWise the use case (`personal`, `work`, or `business`), your industry, department, and company size.

    This resolves your canonical **domain** (`CUSTOMER_SUCCESS`, `SALES`, `BIZOPS`, `PRODUCT_ENGINEERING`, …) and operating context (`CTX_CS`, `CTX_BIZOPS`, `CTX_SALES`, …). Both drive what projections you'll see in step 4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goals">
    Name your workspace and pick a goal. The goal seeds the Think reasoning tool and the Triage inbox so signals are scored against what you actually care about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Authorize one or more tools through Nango. Each connector is tagged Flow A, B, or C:

    - **Flow A** — structured truth (CRM, billing, support, finance, project systems)
    - **Flow B** — unstructured context (documents, emails, notes, files)
    - **Flow C** — interaction surfaces that require human triage

    The 8-stage pipeline starts normalizing data into the Spine immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens behind the scenes

While you finish onboarding, the system runs through five constitutional phases so your desk is populated, not blank:

| Phase | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Spine hydration** | Real data flows through the 8-stage pipeline and writes the Spine (D1 partitions). |
| **Memory activation** | Conversational, org, and personal memory begin accumulating through the continuity pipeline. |
| **Projection generation** | The Spine Context yields nav, modules, entity views, and metrics for your domain. |
| **Governance activation** | The HARD GATE comes online — AI may think and propose, but never act without an approval token. |
| **Continuity activation** | Context is shared across every AI, tool, and session — the IW Continuity Bridge is on. |

## Arriving at your desk

When `spineInitialized` flips to `true`, you land on your **L1 workbench** for your domain (for example, the Customer Success workbench for `CTX_CS`). From there:

- Press `⌘K` to open the command palette for live Spine entity search and quick actions.
- Press `⌘J` to open the L2 cognitive drawer — Think reasoning, Triage inbox, signals, and approvals.
- Review and approve proposed actions. Approved actions hand you a client-side executable **Playbook (JSON)** to run locally, so credentials never leave your environment.

## Connect more tools

You can add connectors any time from the workspace. Each new tool extends the same Spine — there is no per-tool silo, no separate workspace, and no duplicate ingestion.
