This quickstart walks you through creating a tenant, picking a domain, and connecting your first tool. By the end, your workspace is pre-filled with live data from your own systems and the Twin has the context it needs to start surfacing signals.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- A work email or a Google/GitHub account
- Admin access to at least one tool you want to connect (for example, a CRM, support desk, or billing system)
- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari)

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [app.integratewise.ai](https://app.integratewise.ai) and sign in with Google or GitHub. Email/password sign-in is gated and off by default — OAuth is the live entry path.

    On first sign-in, IntegrateWise atomically creates your tenant, your user, and your Customer Zero workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your domain">
    Choose the work domain that matches your role. Your L1 Workbench, modules, and data partitions all snap to this choice.

    - **Customer Success** — accounts, health, renewals, escalations
    - **Sales** — pipeline, opportunities, forecast
    - **RevOps** — funnel, attribution, lifecycle
    - **BizOps** — cross-functional metrics and projects
    - **Personal** — always available alongside your work domain

    You can only have one work domain active at a time. Personal is always on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your first tool">
    From the onboarding flow, authorize one connector — CRM, support desk, billing, messaging, or documents. OAuth runs through the Gateway and writes credentials to the encrypted vault.

    As soon as the connector lands, the **8-stage pipeline** classifies, normalizes, and writes the data into the Spine. Your workspace stops being empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See it work">
    Open your domain workbench. You should now see:

    - Entity 360 cards (accounts, contacts, deals, tickets) sourced from the Spine
    - The L2 Cognitive Overlay (`⌘J`) with early signals and insights
    - The Twin (`⌘K` for commands, dedicated workbench for chat) with grounded context from your data

    Ask the Twin a question about a specific account or opportunity — it reads across every connected system and shows the evidence behind its answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve your first action">
    The Twin never acts alone. When it proposes an action — a CRM update, a follow-up task, a workflow — the proposal lands in the **Approval Center** with full context and evidence.

    Review, approve, and choose how to dispatch: export as a JSON playbook, hand off to Hermes, OpenClaw, or MCP. Execution happens in your own environment; the Spine records the outcome and learns from it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What just happened

Behind the scenes, your one click of "connect" triggered the full IntegrateWise loop:

`Connect tools → Data flows in → Pipeline normalizes → Spine stores truth → Intelligence surfaces what matters → You review and approve → Action executes → System learns`

Every cycle makes the next one smarter. The AI learns from your decisions. The Spine grows with your business.
