# Integration, Plugin & MCP Marketplaces

SpineWorkspace treats marketplaces as capability discovery and distribution surfaces.

## Marketplace family

- Integration Marketplace
- Plugin Marketplace
- MCP Marketplace

## Common lifecycle

```text
Discover
 ↓
Validate
 ↓
Install / Connect
 ↓
Authorize
 ↓
Register Capability
 ↓
Govern
 ↓
Execute
```

Marketplace availability does not grant authority. Plugins and MCP servers do not become owners of canonical Business Context.
