# Spine Fabric

Spine Fabric is the contextual business memory and operational continuity substrate of SpineWorkspace.

It connects organizational entities, work, events, decisions, evidence, relationships, current state and governed memory.

```text
Source Systems
 ↓
Ingestion & Synthesis
 ↓
Spine Fabric
 ↓
Context / Projection
 ↓
Human + AI Work
 ↓
Reconciliation
 ↓
Updated Context
```

AI does not directly own canonical Spine. AI works through bounded operational context.
