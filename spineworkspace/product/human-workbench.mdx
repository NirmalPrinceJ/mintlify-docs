# Human Workbench

The Human Workbench is the user's operational surface for understanding, deciding on and progressing Work.

## Surface model

```text
Business Context
 ↓
Projection
 ↓
Human Workbench
 ↓
Current State / Context / Work / Decisions / Communications
```

## Information architecture

- Personal
- Today
- My Work
- My Queue
- Communications
- Organizational Memory
- Work
- Business-domain work surfaces

## Adaptive projection

The Workbench is projected from organization, role, domain, responsibility, authority, permissions, current Work, Business Context and policy.

## Boundary

The Workbench is a projection, not a second source of truth.
