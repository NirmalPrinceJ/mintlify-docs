Governance is not a setting — it is a structural property of IntegrateWise. The runtime is wired so that AI cannot mutate the outside world without a signed approval token.

## The HARD GATE

```
Signal / change  →  Intelligence (Think)  →  Proposal
   →  S8 Governance (HARD GATE)
   →  Act  →  writeback  →  signal
```

- No execution without an `x-approval-token`. The Act service rejects unsigned proposals at the boundary.
- HITL (Human-in-the-Loop) is mandatory for write-backs to connected tools.
- Promotion to `org_memory` is human-approved through the L7 Governance Workbench.
- The Twin is **read-only** to the Spine. Memory writes go through the continuity pipeline only.

## What "approval" means in practice

When the Twin proposes an action — close a ticket, update a deal stage, draft an outbound email — three things happen:

1. **Proposal** — the Twin emits a structured proposal with the evidence behind it (which Spine entities and signals informed the decision).
2. **Approval Center** — the proposal lands in your queue with a diff of the intended writeback. You can approve, modify, or reject.
3. **Playbook handoff** — approved actions become a client-side executable **Playbook (JSON)**. Credentials never leave your environment; the writeback runs locally and reports back to the Spine, which emits a fresh signal.

## Tenant isolation

- Every Spine plane (entities, relationships, signals, memory) is tenant-isolated through `tenant_id`.
- Downstream workers are not exposed to the public internet — they route through internal Cloudflare service bindings.
- All public traffic enters through the Gateway at `gateway.integratewise.ai`, which enforces JWT + tenant headers before any downstream service is touched.
- Every Spine access is audit-logged in `spine_audit_log`.

## Why this matters

The same context that makes the Twin powerful — full read access across every connected system — is exactly what makes ungoverned AI dangerous. The HARD GATE means:

- AI **never** writes to a connected tool without explicit, per-action human approval.
- Credentials for connected tools never leave the user side at action time.
- Every action is auditable, with the evidence chain preserved in the Spine.
