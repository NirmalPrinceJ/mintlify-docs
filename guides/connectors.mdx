A connector is the bridge between a third-party tool (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Stripe, Jira, Google Drive, ...) and the Spine. Connectors are authorized through Nango and tagged by the data flow they carry.

## The three connector flows

| Flow | Carries | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flow A** | Structured truth — authoritative records | HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe, Jira, QuickBooks |
| **Flow B** | Unstructured context — documents, emails, notes | Google Drive, Notion, Gmail, Slack |
| **Flow C** | AI interactions — sessions and agents | Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Perplexity |

## Adding a connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connectors panel">
    From the workbench, open **Settings → Connectors**, or run the OAuth flow during onboarding's **Connect** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start OAuth">
    Click the provider. The frontend calls `GET /api/v1/connectors/:provider/authorize` and redirects you to the provider's consent screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete OAuth">
    The provider redirects to `GET /api/v1/connectors/:provider/callback`. The callback persists the token, registers the connector, and tags its flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hydration starts">
    The Loader begins enqueuing raw events. The 8-stage pipeline normalizes them and writes canonical entities to the Spine. You can watch progress in the workbench's freshness indicators.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to your data

Once a connector is live:

- Inbound webhooks and scheduled pulls enqueue raw events through `services/connector`.
- The 8-stage pipeline classifies each event, runs identity resolution, and writes to the right D1 partition.
- Existing canonical entities are merged where possible (HubSpot `Contact` + Salesforce `Lead` for the same person → one canonical Person).
- The workbench picks up the changes through projections; the Twin can immediately reason over the new data.

## Identity resolution and merges

When two records look like the same real-world thing but the system isn't sure, they show up as **merge candidates**:

| Endpoint | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/identity/candidates` | List merge candidates |
| `POST /api/v1/identity/candidates/:id/merge` | Merge into one canonical entity |
| `POST /api/v1/identity/candidates/:id/keep-separate` | Mark as not duplicates |
| `POST /api/v1/identity/candidates/:id/defer` | Defer the decision |
| `GET /api/v1/identity/stats` | Resolution statistics |

## Governance reminder

Connectors are read-and-write surfaces, but writes always go through the [HARD GATE](/concepts/governance). The Twin proposes; you approve; the approved Playbook runs client-side and reports back.
