IntegrateWise is the **IW Continuity Bridge** — a shared, normalized, governed knowledge workspace over the Spine, empowered by AI. Your tools finally talk to each other, AI gets the full picture, and you stop being the integration between your own systems.

The moment you connect your first app, your real data flows in. Your workspace arrives pre-filled, not empty. AI reads across every connected system, surfaces what matters — risks, opportunities, patterns — with the evidence behind every insight. And it never acts alone: every recommendation waits for your approval.

## Why IntegrateWise

Every team has 15+ tools. None of them talk to each other. You become the integration layer — cross-referencing, reconciling, deciding, then manually updating each system. IntegrateWise replaces that burden with a unified intelligence layer that:

- **Connects** — ingests and normalizes data from every tool
- **Unifies** — resolves entities into a single source of truth (the Spine)
- **Understands** — surfaces signals, insights, and context
- **Remembers** — maintains organizational and personal memory
- **Reasons** — proposes actions and workflows through the Twin
- **Governs** — every action is approved before it leaves the system
- **Hands off** — packages execution into a vendor-neutral contract you run in your own environment
