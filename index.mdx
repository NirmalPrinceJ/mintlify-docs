## What IntegrateWise is

IntegrateWise is the **IW Continuity Bridge** — a shared, normalized, governed knowledge workspace over the Spine. It connects the tools your team already uses (CRM, support, finance, messaging, documents, and AI assistants) into one live, intelligent workspace.

The moment you connect your first app, real data flows in. Your workspace arrives pre-filled, not empty. AI reads across every connected system simultaneously and surfaces what matters — risks, opportunities, patterns — with the evidence behind every insight.

**AI never acts alone.** Every recommendation waits for your approval.

## The core loop

```
Connect tools → Data flows in → Engine normalizes → Spine stores truth
  → Intelligence surfaces what matters → User reviews and approves
  → Action executes → System learns
```

Every cycle makes the next one smarter. The Spine grows with your business.
