IntegrateWise is a shared, normalized, governed knowledge workspace over the Spine, empowered by AI. The moment you connect your first app, your real data flows in. Your workspace arrives pre-filled, not empty. AI reads across every connected system, surfaces what matters — risks, opportunities, patterns — with evidence behind every insight. And it never acts alone: every recommendation waits for your approval.

You don't replace your tools. You finally make them work together.

## The core loop

`Connect tools → Data flows in → Engine normalizes → Spine stores truth → Intelligence surfaces what matters → You review and approve → Action executes → System learns`

Every cycle makes the next one smarter. AI learns from your decisions. The Spine grows with your business.

## Two perspectives

**The user surface.** A guided onboarding flow, domain workbenches for Customer Success, Sales, RevOps, and BizOps, a `⌘K` command palette for live entity search, and a `⌘J` cognitive drawer with the Think reasoning tool, Triage inbox, and approvals.

**The systems engine.** A Cloudflare-native serverless backend: secure ingestion, an 8-stage normalization pipeline, an edge memory plane (D1 + KV), a Supabase fortress database, and zero-trust service bindings — all behind a single Gateway.
